# How a Swedish auditor actually audits an SME aktiebolag: research report

Compiled 2026-09-12 by a research agent with web verification. Sources linked inline. Licensing note at the end governs what the persona may quote.

## 1. Binding standards and law

**Hierarchy.** Revisorslagen (2001:883) §19 requires god revisorssed / god revisionssed; FAR states that ISA (and now ISA for LCE) is the basis of god revisionssed, that Swedish specifics are regulated by FAR through RevR/RevU, that Revisorsinspektionen (RI) interprets it, and that ultimately courts decide (FAR, Inledning till regler om revision: https://www.faronline.se/dokument/far/revr/revrinledning/ ; Revisorslag: https://lagen.nu/2001:883, §21 independence, §21a analysmodellen).

**ISA as applied in Sweden.** FAR publishes Swedish translations with svenska tillägg on FAR Online (paywalled; previews only, e.g. https://www.faronline.se/dokument/isa/isa/isa240/ , https://www.faronline.se/dokument/isa/isa/isa320/). English originals are free in the IAASB 2025 Handbook (personal, non-commercial use): https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services . SME working set: ISA 200, 210, 220, 230, 240, 250, 260/265, 300, 315 (Revised 2019), 320, 330, 450, 500, 501, 505, 520, 530, 540, 550, 560, 570, 580, 700/705/706/710/720, plus ISQM 1 at firm level.

**ISA for LCE.** RI's general statement: ISA for LCE counts as god revisionssed for eligible entities, financial years beginning after 15 Dec 2025 (early adoption allowed); barred for listed companies, credit/insurance firms and större företag/koncerner under ÅRL (https://www.faronline.se/nyheter/2024/oktober/nytt-uttalande-fran-ri-om-isa-for-mindre-komplexa-foretag/ ; https://www.far.se/kunskap/revision/isa-for-lce/). Most Swedish SME audits from FY2026 are ISA-for-LCE audits.

**RevR/RevU (FAR), all paywalled.** RevR 209 Förvaltningsrevision, substantially revised, in force 2025-01-01, explicit risk assessment and links to ISA 240/250 (https://www.far.se/aktuellt/nyheter/2024/september/uppdaterad-rekommendation-om-forvaltningsrevision/ ; https://www.faronline.se/dokument/far/revr/revr0209/). RevR 700 audit-report wording (https://www.faronline.se/dokument/far/revr/revr0700/). RevR 705 modified opinions, emphasis paragraphs, anmärkningar (https://www.faronline.se/dokument/far/revr/revr0705/). RevR 14 suspected crime and money laundering (https://www.faronline.se/dokument/far/revr/revr0014/). RevR 20 Revisionsberättelser för mindre komplexa företag, published 2026-04-27, replaces RevR 700/705 under ISA for LCE (https://www.far.se/aktuellt/nyheter/2026/april/revr-20-ger-stod-vid-revisionsberattelser-enligt-isa-for-lce/ ; https://www.far.se/aktuellt/nyheter/2026/januari/andringar-i-fars-rekommendationer-och-uttalanden-inom-revision/).

**Aktiebolagslagen (2005:551), free** (https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/ ; https://lagen.nu/2005:551): 9:3 audit of accounts, bookkeeping and the board's/CEO's administration; 9:31–35 report content, ansvarsfrihet, resultatdisposition, 9:33 anmärkning for acts breaching ABL/ÅRL/bolagsordning, 9:34 anmärkning on taxes and charges; 9:37 copy to Skatteverket; 9:42–44 crime reporting; 17:3 försiktighetsregeln; 21:1–2 loan prohibition and exceptions; 25:13 kontrollbalansräkning when equity is below half the share capital.

**Revisorsinspektionen.** Risk-based supervision, quality control, thematic supervision, förhandsbesked; sanctions erinran, varning (optionally with sanktionsavgift), upphävande (https://www.revisorsinspektionen.se/tillsyn/). Anonymised decisions since 1995, free: https://www.revisorsinspektionen.se/praxissok/ . Tillsynsnämnden summaries on RI's news page (e.g. https://www.revisorsinspektionen.se/publikationer/nyheter/2026/tillsynsnamnden-for-revisorer-sammantradde-den-20-februari-2026-och-fattade-fem-beslut/).

## 3. Method in practice

**Risk assessment (ISA 315).** Understand entity, owner-manager, IT and controls; in SMEs controls are weak and the auditor takes a substantive approach. RI's exam answer: identify the primary users (owner, bank) before setting materiality.

**Materiality (ISA 320).** FAR: profit before tax 3–7 % (up to 10 % common in private companies), revenue/costs 1–3 %, assets 1–3 %, equity 3–5 %; performance materiality 60–85 % of overall; in small entities benchmarks can give unreasonably low values and a higher documented amount is acceptable (https://www.far.se/kunskap/revision/vasentlighet-i-revisionen/). RI expects lowering the percentage when profitability falls, covenants tighten or ownership widens.

**Standard programme per area** (ISA 500/501/505/520/530, IFAC guide): receivables: ageing, subsequent receipts, confirmations (ISA 505), bad-debt reserve. Payables: supplier statements, unrecorded liabilities search after year-end. Inventory: attend count (ISA 501), price test, obsolescence. Fixed assets: register, additions vouching, depreciation per K2/K3, impairment. Equity: share register, bolagsstämmoprotokoll, ABL 25:13 test. Revenue: cut-off, completeness (especially cash businesses), analytics. Payroll: reconcile AGI filings to 7xxx accounts, benefits. Tax/VAT: skattekonto statement, VAT analytics against sales/purchases, INK2 reconciliation. RI's model answer lists the förvaltningsrevision programme: bolagsordning, insurance, cash handling, minutes, BFL compliance by voucher sampling, creditor protection (värdeöverföringar, kontrollbalansräkning, förbjudna lån), and reviewing the skattekonto statement for late payments.

**Analytical procedures (ISA 520).** Balans experts: analytics are rarely sufficient as the only procedure and can miss fictitious revenue, wrong amounts and cut-off; in cash-based SMEs the fraud risk is under-reporting revenue for tax, a completeness risk (https://tidningenbalans.se/artikel/expertfragan-sa-kan-granskning-av-intakter-bli-mer-traffsaker).

**Related parties and the loan ban (ISA 550, ABL 21).** Loans to shareholders, directors, CEO and their närstående are prohibited (exceptions: koncernlån, commercial loans, holdings under 1 %). RI's exam answer: a repaid, market-rate loan is still a breach of ABL and must be remarked on, though it need not affect ansvarsfrihet if no damage arose.

**Going concern (ISA 570) and subsequent events (ISA 560).** Tied to ABL 25:13; check whether a kontrollbalansräkning was required and drawn up; disclose material uncertainty per RevR 705.

**Fraud (ISA 240).** Presumed revenue risk, management override, journal-entry testing; the Swedish appendix uses Incitament/tryck, Möjligheter, Inställning/ursäkter.

**Förvaltningsrevision (RevR 209).** Covers ansvarsfrihet, breaches of ABL/ÅRL/bolagsordning, taxes and charges, and the reasonableness of the dividend proposal against 17:3 (https://www.faronline.se/dokument/balans-fordjupning/2020-tal/2024/balans_fordjupning_2024111301/).

## 4. Reporting

The revisionsberättelse has two parts: Rapport om årsredovisningen and Rapport om andra krav enligt lagar och andra författningar (RevR 700/705). The second carries the opinions on resultatdisposition and ansvarsfrihet and any anmärkning. Common anmärkningar: late or unpaid taxes/AGI (interest, reminder fees, skattetillägg on the skattekonto are the tell-tales), late annual report/AGM beyond six months, förbjudet lån, missing kontrollbalansräkning (https://www.revideco.se/ekonomiansvarig/anmarkningar-i-revisionsberattelsen/). A qualified report must be sent without delay to Skatteverket in eight enumerated situations (ABL 9:37; https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/etjansterochblanketter/svarpavanligafragor/fleromraden/narskakopiapaorenrevisionsberattelseskickastillskatteverket.5.71004e4c133e23bf6db8000111796.html); it is public at Bolagsverket, typically zeroes credit ratings.

## 5. Sources on typical SME errors

- RI discipline 2020–24: 205 cases (73 erinran, 124 varning, 8 upphävande); root cause per the chair: auditors relied uncritically on client information without sufficient professional skepticism; hot areas are revenue, material balance items, documentation (https://tidningenbalans.se/artikel/darfor-blir-revisorer-fallda-har-ar-de-vanligaste-bristerna). Feb 2025 batch: inventory without count evidence or obsolescence assessment, revenue completeness, unflagged prohibited loan, duplicate reports (https://tidningenbalans.se/artikel/atta-revisorer-varnas-av-revisorsinspektionen ; https://www.far.se/aktuellt/nyheter/2025/februari/far-kommenterar-revisorsinspektionens-senaste-tillsynsarenden/).
- Skatteverket/Företagarna: private costs in the company, unreported car benefits, input VAT on private purchases, wrong VAT periods, late declarations (https://www.foretagarna.se/driva-eget-foretag/deklaration-och-bokslut/vanligaste-felen-smaforetagare-gor-i-bokslut-och-deklaration/).
- Bookkeeping basics: BFNAR 2013:2 (vouchers, timeliness, archiving) is what bokföringsbrott cases hinge on (https://www.bfn.se/wp-content/uploads/vl13-2-bokforing.pdf).
- Crime reporting: EBM/FAR guidance lists the trigger crimes (BrB 9:1,3,9; 10:1,3,4,5,5a–e; 11:1,2,4,5; SkBrL 2,4,5,10) (https://www.ekobrottsmyndigheten.se/wp-content/uploads/se/2022/08/revisorsanmalan.pdf ; https://www.ekobrottsmyndigheten.se/om-ekobrott/anmal-ekobrott/revisor/).

## Implications for an AI auditor persona

**Ten questions, in order, with source:**
1. Who uses these accounts and what do they care about (owner, bank, Skatteverket)? ISA 320; RI exam.
2. Am I independent and is the firm's quality system in place? RevL §21/21a, ISQM 1, ISA 220.
3. Is the business, ownership and finance function less complex, and which framework (K2/K3, ISA vs ISA for LCE)? ISA 315, ISA for LCE Part A, RI statement.
4. Where can revenue be incomplete or fictitious, and can management override controls? ISA 240, Balans expert article.
5. What is materiality and performance materiality, and does the benchmark give a sensible figure? ISA 320, FAR guidance.
6. Does bank (1930) reconcile, and do the skattekonto and AGI/VAT filings show late payments or penalties? RevR 209, ABL 9:34.
7. Are there loans or transactions with owners, directors or their relatives? ISA 550, ABL 21:1.
8. Is equity above half the share capital and can the company pay its debts for twelve months? ISA 570, ABL 25:13, 17:3.
9. For each material balance (inventory, receivables, fixed assets) what did I see (count, confirmation, subsequent receipt), not just what I was told? ISA 500/501/505/530; RI discipline pattern.
10. What happened after year-end, and did I get written representations and document it all? ISA 560/580/230.

**Where the auditor must act, not merely note:** anmärkning for tax/charge failures and ABL/ÅRL/bolagsordning breaches (ABL 9:33–34); copy to Skatteverket without delay (9:37); notify the board and, within four weeks, the prosecutor on suspicion of the listed crimes unless remedied, already reported or insignificant (9:42–44, RevR 14); money-laundering reporting overrides all (RevR 14 §1.3); act on a missing kontrollbalansräkning; refuse fixed-fee assurance work (EtikU 3).
