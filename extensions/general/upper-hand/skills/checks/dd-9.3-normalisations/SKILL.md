# Normalisation list (9.3)

**Looks for.** Costs and revenues in the last 24 months that do not reflect underlying performance: moving costs, legal fees in a dispute, settlements and damages, fines, severance and restructuring, start-up and establishment costs, recruitment fees, repairs after a supplier fault, insurance compensation, rent rebates, write-downs, clean-up fees from a previous bookkeeper, corrections of prior years, and the advisers of the transaction itself (M&A, legal, DD, audit beyond the statutory audit). One line per invoice. Each line classed one-off, DD-specific or possible recovery.

**Data from Accounted.** `accounted_list_supplier_invoices` (status all, date_from 24 months back, paginate; returns supplier, number, dates, total, total_sek, currency and vat_treatment but not line text). `accounted_query_journal` for line text and accounts: source_type supplier_invoice_registered for the invoice vouchers, source_type manual on 5xxx to 7xxx and 39xx for costs and other income booked without a supplier invoice. `accounted_list_suppliers` for category and country. `accounted_get_income_statement` per fiscal period for the base. `accounted_list_verifikat_without_documents` to know which candidates have no document behind them.

**Rule.** Candidate when any of: (a) the supplier has at most two invoices in 24 months and the invoice is 10 000 kr or more excl. VAT; (b) line or voucher text matches one-off vocabulary: flytt, advokat, jurist, ombud, tvist, förlikning, skadestånd, vite, avgångsvederlag, uppsägning, omstrukturering, uppstart, etablering, rekrytering, transaktion, due diligence, DD, rådgivning inför försäljning, försäkringsersättning, reparation efter, garanti, nedskrivning, hyresrabatt, uppstädning, rättelse tidigare år; (c) a line on 39xx (other operating income) of 10 000 kr or more. A candidate becomes a line when an invoice or document is behind it and it does not repeat at the same cadence in the prior year. Severity `info` for the list itself: it is a deliverable, not an error. `attention` when a candidate of 10 000 kr or more has no invoice or document behind it, because it cannot be defended to the buyer, or when the lines together exceed 10 % of the year's result before financial items. When no candidate survives, report "Ingen avvikelse" with the population checked: number of suppliers, number of invoices, period and threshold.

**False-alarm guard.** A cost that repeats at the same cadence in the prior year is run-rate, not a one-off: monthly rent and software, quarterly usage invoices from the same vendor, monthly travel, bank and payment fees, salaries, social fees and pension all stay in EBITDA however they are labelled. A "one-off" that appears every quarter belongs in run-rate; say so in the evidence. Every line needs an invoice or a document; a candidate without one is reported as "underlag saknas", not put on the list. A purchase capitalised on 12xx is an asset and its depreciation is underlying; it is not a one-off however large the invoice. The transaction's own advisers, the seller's and the reviewer's, are DD-specific: a separate class, never mixed with operational one-offs. A booking error found by another check, such as a doubled payment or wrong VAT, is a correction, not a normalisation: mention it under "Övrigt att titta på" and leave the finding to that check. A recoverable item (warranty repair, insurance claim) is listed as "möjlig återvinning" only when a claim exists or is plausible from the document, never as "kommer att ersättas". Foreign-currency invoices use total_sek from the invoice; name that as the source. Items under 10 000 kr excl. VAT are ignored unless several from the same supplier or the same event sum above it. Never take a normalisation from the seller's pack without finding the invoice behind it.

**Cites.** swedish-year-end-closing (jämförelsestörande poster, nedskrivning, periodisering), swedish-accounting-compliance (a verification behind every business event), swedish-financial-reporting (disclosure of exceptional items in K2 and K3).

**Evidence.** Per line: supplier, supplier invoice id and number, invoice date, service text, amount excl. VAT and incl. VAT (total_sek), account, voucher id, document id or "underlag saknas", class. Totals per supplier and per class. The year's result before financial items from the income statement as the base, with the fiscal period id.

**What closes it.** The list accepted by the CFO with an invoice behind every line and the class of each line agreed with the buyer's advisor; lines without an invoice removed or documented. Owner: CFO. Date: before the quality-of-earnings delivery.

**Situation.** sale. **Persona.** dd-analyst, next-cfo.

**Case title (sv).** "Normaliseringslista: {count} poster, {amount_excl} kr exkl. moms, varav {missing} utan underlag"
**Pattern deviated from (sv).** "Justerad EBITDA bygger på engångsposter som var och en har en faktura bakom sig och inte återkommer. Här saknar {missing} poster underlag, och {recurring} poster återkommer i samma takt som föregående år."
