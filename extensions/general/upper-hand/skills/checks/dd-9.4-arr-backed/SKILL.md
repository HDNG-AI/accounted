# Recurring revenue backed by schedule, contract and payment (9.4)

**Looks for.** Customer invoice series that the books present as recurring: item text with subscription vocabulary (abonnemang, prenumeration, subscription, licens, månadsavgift, retainer, "månad N/YYYY"), or the same customer invoiced the same total at monthly or shorter cadence for three or more consecutive periods with quantity 1 and no hours or unit basis on the line. For each series: is there a recurring invoice schedule for the customer, is there an agreement document in the archive, were the invoices paid and within terms, and what share of the period's revenue does the series carry.

**Data from Accounted.** `accounted_list_invoices` without status filter, paginated with offset until has_more is false; it returns customer, total, dates and status but not items or paid amounts. Then `accounted_get_invoice` on one or two invoices per series for items (description, quantity, unit, unit_price, labor_hours), paid_amount, remaining_amount and credited_invoice_id. `accounted_list_recurring_schedules` for schedules per customer; this tool is search-only, so if it is not in your tool list find it with `accounted_search_tools` (keyword "abonnemang") or call it through `accounted_call_tool`. `accounted_list_unmatched_documents` and the document ids on the customer's vouchers for agreement documents, matched on customer name. `accounted_list_customers` for payment terms and country. `accounted_get_income_statement` or `accounted_get_kpi_report` for revenue in the period.

**Rule.** For every series presented as recurring: hit when no schedule (active or paused) exists for the customer and no agreement document for the customer is found in the archive. Severity `attention` when every invoice in the series is paid within terms: the revenue is real, the recurring claim is undocumented. `blocking` when in addition any invoice in the series is unpaid past due, or the series stopped without a credit note or termination document while later periods exist in the ledger, or the series carries more than 25 % of the period's revenue. `info` when a schedule exists but no agreement document: the buyer will still ask for the contract. Series that pass go in the same table with schedule id, document id and payment history, so the buyer sees what was verified, not only what failed.

**False-alarm guard.** Klient AB is the positive case and must not be flagged: weekly invoices of 36 000 kr whose lines read 24 h at 1 200 kr, paid within two weeks throughout 2025. The revenue basis is the delivered hours on the invoice itself, so it is time-and-materials revenue backed by delivery and payment, with or without a schedule. Its 2026 arrears belong to `dd-9.7-aging`, not here. A project invoiced in instalments or per workshop with varying amounts (Berlin GmbH) is project revenue: not recurring, not an error, listed as such. Two invoices are not a series; three consecutive periods minimum. A contract can live outside the system: write "inget avtal i arkivet", never state as a fact that no agreement exists. The first invoice of a new subscription may precede the signed agreement by days. A paused schedule is still a schedule; report the pause. Follow credited_invoice_id so a credited and reissued invoice counts once. Zero VAT on export or reverse-charge invoices is not a signal of anything here. When customer invoicing stops for all customers months before today, the books are not current: state the last invoiced month, measure every series to that month, and do not read the stop as churn. Never derive recurring revenue from a spreadsheet or from the seller's pack; only from invoices returned by the tools.

**Cites.** swedish-invoice-compliance (invoice content, credit notes), swedish-financial-reporting (revenue recognition, disclosure of significant customers), swedish-accounting-compliance (a document behind every business event).

**Evidence.** Per series: customer, invoice ids and numbers, invoice dates, totals, item text, paid_at or remaining_amount per invoice, schedule id or "inget schema", agreement document id or "inget avtal i arkivet", the series' share of the period's revenue, and the last invoiced month in the ledger.

**What closes it.** The signed agreement uploaded to the archive and linked to the customer, or a recurring schedule created by the company with the agreement referenced in its name or notes. Or a written statement in the data room that the revenue is project revenue, with the customer's order behind it. Owner: CEO or CFO. Date: before the next data-room delivery.

**Situation.** sale. **Persona.** dd-analyst, controller.

**Case title (sv).** "Återkommande intäkt utan avtal eller schema: {customer}, {amount} kr per månad, {count} fakturor"
**Pattern deviated from (sv).** "Återkommande intäkt styrks av avtal eller faktureringsschema och av betalningar i tid. Här finns fakturorna och betalningarna, men varken avtal i arkivet eller schema."
